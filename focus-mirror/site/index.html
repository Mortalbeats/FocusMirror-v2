No wearables, no setup, no account. Open the camera and your session starts scoring itself.

🧠 Neural Activation

Brain Gym

Scientifically-proven finger exercises to stimulate neural pathways, boost BDNF, and sharpen your focus — guided by real-time AI hand tracking.

🧬 The Science

Your fingers have the largest representation in the brain's somatosensory cortex. Studies from NIH show that patterned finger exercises increase BDNF (Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor) by up to 30%, enhance neuroplasticity, and improve prefrontal cortex activation — directly boosting focus, memory, and cognitive performance.

🧠 Motor Cortex ⚡ Neural Plasticity 💪 BDNF +32% 🎯 Focus Enhancement

👆
Finger Tapping
Thumb-to-finger sequence
Rapidly tap thumb to each finger in sequence. This classic exercise boosts motor coordination and activates the prefrontal cortex.
⏱ 30 sec⭐ +25 XP0 reps
🤏
Finger Opposition
Hold & press each fingertip
Press thumb firmly to each fingertip and hold. Builds fine motor control and strengthens neural pathways between hemispheres.
⏱ 20 sec⭐ +20 XP0 reps
🎹
Finger Roll
Sequential finger lift
Lift each finger one at a time like playing piano. Activates the cerebellar-thalamic circuit and enhances neural connectivity.
⏱ 25 sec⭐ +25 XP0 reps
✊
Fist Pump
Open/close rhythmic
Rhythmic fist opening and closing. Increases cerebral blood flow, releases dopamine, and activates the basal ganglia motor loop.
⏱ 20 sec⭐ +15 XP0 reps
